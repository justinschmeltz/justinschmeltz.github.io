02 / My path

A learning career built around performance.

My career began in education, where the work was never just delivering content. It was figure out why someone is stuck, design the right support, facilitate it well, and use evidence to decide what comes next.

That foundation now shapes how I approach workplace learning: start with the performance problem, respect the learner, make the experience useful, and measure whether it changed anything that matters.

Foundation

Learning science in real environments

Years of designing for diverse learners built a practical instinct for accessibility, differentiation, feedback, and transfer—not as add-ons, but as part of the design.

Systems thinking

From individual needs to coordinated support

Special education added a deeper layer: diagnose barriers, work across stakeholders, build targeted supports, and continuously adjust based on evidence.

Emerging technology

AI changed the questions I wanted to solve

My interest shifted from simply using new tools to helping people develop the judgment, confidence, and habits to use them well.

Current focus

Learning & Development at workplace scale

I apply that human-centered, evidence-driven approach to L&D, enablement, and AI fluency: clarify the performance need, build useful support, and define how improvement would be measured.

03 / About me

Curious about people. Serious about useful learning.

I’m a San Jose–based L&D professional focused on learning strategy, facilitation, performance support, accessibility, and AI-enabled learning. My background in general and special education sharpened how I diagnose performance barriers, partner across stakeholders, and design clear support for varied learners.

I want the work I design to feel clear, approachable, and worth someone’s time—not training for training’s sake.
